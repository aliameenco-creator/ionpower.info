Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the ION POWER website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data (memory files) stored on your computer or device via your browser by sites you visit. They can last for a single visit (“session cookies”) or for repeat visits (“persistent cookies”). Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way.

Cookies we use

Visitors. Browsing this website does not place analytics or advertising cookies on your device, and the contact form works without cookies.

Site administrators. WordPress sets strictly necessary cookies (such as wordpress_logged_in_*, wordpress_sec_* and wp-settings-*) only when an administrator signs in to manage the website. They are needed for that purpose and do not require consent.

Embedded video. The video “The Process of Cavitation, explained” is provided by YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the video is loaded from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube (Google) may store cookies or similar data on your device under its own privacy policy.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies in their settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect how websites work. These pages explain how to manage cookies in the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome

To find out more about cookies, visit aboutcookies.org.

Cavitation

Cavitation

Cavitation occurs when the pressure within a liquid falls below its vapour pressure, creating microscopic vapour-filled cavities. When these cavities enter a higher-pressure region, they collapse rapidly and release concentrated mechanical energy through shock waves, microjets and localised heating.

Cavitation is normally associated with erosion and damage in pumps, turbines and propellers. ION POWER is developing technology designed to control and harness this process as a source of continuous mechanical and electrical power.

ION POWER harnesses controlled cavitation to produce baseload power, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

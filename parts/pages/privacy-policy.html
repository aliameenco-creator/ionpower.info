Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ION POWER (“ION POWER”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects and uses personal data when you visit this website or contact us through it. ION POWER is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. If you have any question about it, please reach us through our Contact Us page.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form we collect your name, your email address and your message. If we continue the conversation, we also keep the details you choose to share with us, such as your organisation, role, phone number, the location of a planned site or your power requirements.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical data when pages are requested: IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested, the referring page and the date and time. We use it to keep the website secure and working.

Cookies. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details, including the video player.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and follow up on it, including commercial, technical, project, partnership and investor enquiries. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To evaluate potential projects when you share site or energy information with us. Lawful basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, or our legitimate interests.
  • To operate and protect the website, for example to prevent spam and abuse. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To comply with the law, for example record-keeping, regulatory requests and the defence of legal claims. Lawful basis: legal obligation or legitimate interests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling. We only send you updates about ION POWER if you ask us to, and you can stop them at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Our website hosting and email service providers, who process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, where necessary.
  • Partners, engineering facilities and affiliated companies involved in answering your enquiry or delivering a project you have asked about, only to the extent needed.
  • Public authorities, regulators or courts when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor if all or part of our business is reorganised or sold, subject to this policy.

International transfers

ION POWER works with partners and facilities in more than one country, including our facility in Turkey. Your data may therefore be processed outside the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. When it is, we use lawful safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another mechanism permitted by applicable law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to answer and follow up on them, and normally no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless we enter into a business relationship or need to keep the information for legal reasons. Technical server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider.

How we protect it

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted administrator access and reputable service providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send confidential technical or commercial information through the contact form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) give you rights to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to our use of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent.

To exercise a right, contact us through our Contact Us page. We will reply within one month and may need to confirm your identity first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK (ico.org.uk), the supervisory authority in your EU country, or the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Turkey.
